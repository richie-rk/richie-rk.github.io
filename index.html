Final Docs refactor

architecture · config-driven dispatch

Final Docs governs how PRMI assembles and delivers post-closing packages to its investor banks, where delays put funding lines and regulatory standing at risk. Onboarding each new investor took 2-4 weeks of bespoke coding and QA, so growing the investor network was capped by engineering capacity, not by business appetite.

  • Creative solution: treat an investor as data, not code. I rebuilt the monolith into config-driven dynamic dispatch: investor-specific logic lives in per-investor YAML definitions mapped to specialized handler functions, so onboarding became a configuration change instead of code surgery.
  • Business value: 4 new investors integrated in a single month, work that previously consumed a full quarter of engineering capacity. Investor expansion now moves at deal speed rather than dev speed, and the architecture was adopted as the template for two other automation initiatives.
