# 0001. Two-door home page

- Status: accepted 30 September 2026, in the portfolio design refactor brief.
- Context: recruiters skim for 30 to 60 seconds, mostly on a phone. At 390 px the home page ran 973 words over 8,147 px, 87% of its body text was muted, and MAX AI and Final Docs sat 1.1 and 2.2 screens down.
- Decision: home becomes the hero, then the deep dives as doors of equal weight side by side, then one quiet "Also shipped" ledger, then contact. Depth moves to the deep dives and the CV.
- Consequences: home is capped at 500 words and 4,500 px at 390 px; the spec lists, bullet points and diagram leave home; every door name sits within 1.5 phone screens of the top.
